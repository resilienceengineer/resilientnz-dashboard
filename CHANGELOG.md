# Dashboard versions

## Infrastructure edition — 20 October 2026

- The main website opens the infrastructure dashboard at `infrastructure/`.
- Roads and highways, water and wastewater, and power infrastructure replace building investment options.
- Six road pathways include illustrative costs, B/C ratios, carbon inventories and weighted scores.
- Auckland utility overlays use published Watercare and Transpower mapping; roads use OpenStreetMap.
- The original dashboard remains available at `original.html`, with its original source files retained.
- Changed scripts and styles receive new content-based URLs to refresh cached versions.
